Questions we often hear

Still unsure? Send us a message on WhatsApp.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
Booking ahead is best, so the doctor can give you unhurried time. Send a WhatsApp message or call 93604 01654 and we'll confirm a slot.
Will I be pushed into surgery?
No. Most joint and back problems improve with medicines, injections or physiotherapy. Surgery is suggested only when it clearly helps — and we explain why.
Can I see the skin doctor and the bone doctor on the same day?
Often, yes. Mention both concerns when you book and we'll try to arrange both consultations together.
What should I bring to my first visit?
Any previous prescriptions, X-rays, MRI or blood reports, and a list of the medicines you take now.
Are skin treatments like lasers and peels safe for Indian skin?
Yes, when chosen and set correctly for your skin type. The dermatologist checks your skin first and starts gently, with clear aftercare.
