Before you visit

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need an appointment?
Booking ahead is best, so the doctor has unhurried time for you. Message or call 93604 01654 and we'll confirm a time.
Will I be pushed into surgery?
No. Most joint and back problems improve with medicines, injections or physiotherapy. Surgery is suggested only when it clearly helps, and you'll be told why.
Can I see both doctors on the same day?
Often, yes. Mention both problems when you book and we'll try to arrange both consultations together.
What should I bring?
Previous prescriptions, X-rays, MRI or blood reports, and a list of the medicines you take now.
Are lasers and peels safe for Indian skin?
Yes, when they're chosen and set for your skin type. The dermatologist checks your skin first, starts gently and gives you clear aftercare.
